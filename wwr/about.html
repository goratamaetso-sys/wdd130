    I live to walk in the purpose Christ has set before me, embracing each day as a gift of grace. With a heart full of wanderlust, I seek adventure not for its own sake, but as a way to marvel at the blessings God has woven into creation. Whether in distant journeys or in the simple joys of daily life, I choose to delight in His presence, to grow through discovery, and to share His love wherever my path leads. My mission is to honor God by living fully, gratefully, and courageously,finding Him in every sunrise, every challenge, and every step of the adventure.

    History

    Born in 1995, I encountered Christ and was baptized in 2009, a turning point that anchored my life in faith. From 2014 to 2018, I pursued studies in Statistics and Psychology, equipping me with tools to understand both numbers and people. In 2021, I entered into marriage, beginning a new chapter of companionship and growth. My entrepreneurial spirit led me to start a scone business in 2022, followed by teaching at a private school from 2023 to 2025, where I nurtured young minds and shared knowledge. At the end of 2025, I embarked on a new academic adventure with BYU-Worldwide, continuing to this day, blending my love for learning with my commitment to living out my purpose in Christ.
