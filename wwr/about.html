Wanderlust Rafting Company

At Wanderlust Rafting Company, our mission is to guide adventurers in experiencing the wonder of God's creation through the thrill of whitewater rafting. We believe each journey on the river is a gift of grace, an opportunity to embrace courage, gratitude, and discovery. With a spirit of adventure, we seek not only excitement in the rapids but also joy in the simple beauty of nature. Our purpose is to honor God by living fully and sharing His love, helping every guest find inspiration in every sunrise, every challenge, and every step of the adventure.

History

Founded in 2009, The Rafting Company began with a vision to combine adventure with purpose. In 2012, we embraced a new chapter that anchored our values in faith and community. From 2014 to 2018, our team expanded its expertise, blending knowledge of people and the environment to create meaningful outdoor experiences. By 2021, we had grown into a close knit company built on trust and companionship. Our entrepreneurial spirit led us to diversify in 2022, and by 2023 we were guiding and teaching adventurers of all ages. At the end of 2025, we launched a new era of growth, continuing to this day with a commitment to learning, discovery, and honoring God through every journey on the river.
